Why I built it

I tried a bunch of relationship tools and bounced off most of them. They either felt like therapy homework, a fluffy quiz, or a spreadsheet pretending to be an app. I wanted something I could run in a few minutes, on my phone, that would turn "vibes and friction" into words we could actually use in a real conversation.

What it does

  • Mobile-first questionnaire: one question at a time, quick tap answers, clear progress.
  • Scores (0–100) across a set of dimensions (attachment, conflict style, regulation, personality traits).
  • Radar chart that highlights the biggest signals so you don't have to read a wall of numbers.
  • One-click export that generates a "coach prompt" you can paste into ChatGPT/Gemini for a plain-English readout.
  • Optional partner run so you can compare results without creating accounts or sharing logins.

Approach

I kept the architecture boring on purpose. Questions live in a local JSON question bank. Answers stay in your browser unless you choose to export them. Scoring is deterministic: each question contributes to one or more vectors (with optional reverse scoring), then everything gets averaged and normalized to 0–100.

  • Question bank: versioned JSON
  • Privacy: localStorage by default (export/share is explicit)
  • Scoring: deterministic mapping from question IDs → vectors
  • Output: readable UI + shareable results + structured "coach" prompt

Who it's for

This is for people who keep having the same conversation (or the same fight) and want a clearer starting point. It works for couples, but it's also useful solo if you're trying to see your own patterns when you're stressed.

For reflection and better conversations, not diagnosis.
