Why I built it

Most relationship tools feel either too clinical, too fluffy, or too complicated. I wanted something fast, private, and useful immediately — a tool that turns “vibes and friction” into concrete language you can actually talk about.

What it does

  • Mobile-first questionnaire: one question at a time with fast tap answers and progress tracking.
  • Trait vectors (0–100): scores map to readable dimensions like attachment, conflict style, regulation, and personality traits.
  • Radar chart (top signals): shows the strongest deviations from neutral to keep the result digestible.
  • Copy/paste coach prompt: exports one block (vectors + instructions) to generate a narrative interpretation in ChatGPT/Gemini.
  • Two-person flow: run a partner session and compare results without accounts/logins.

Approach

The architecture is intentionally simple. Questions live in a local JSON question bank. Answers are stored locally in your browser unless you export/share them. Scoring is deterministic (question sets per vector, optional reverse-scoring, averaged and normalized to 0–100).

  • Question bank: versioned JSON
  • Privacy: localStorage by default (export/share is explicit)
  • Scoring: deterministic mapping from question IDs → vectors
  • Output: readable UI + shareable results + structured “coach” prompt

Who it’s for

People who want a structured mirror and better conversations: couples who keep circling the same pattern, individuals trying to understand their default mode under stress, or anyone who wants something more actionable than “I don’t know, I just felt weird.”

Built for fast insight — not diagnosis.
